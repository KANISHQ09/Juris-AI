MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is effective as of February 1, 2026, between Vertex Corp and Horizon Labs LLC.

1. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" refers to any proprietary technical, financial, or business information disclosed by one party ("Discloser") to the other ("Recipient").

2. NON-DISCLOSURE OBLIGATIONS
Recipient agrees to protect Discloser's Confidential Information with the same degree of care it uses for its own confidential assets, but not less than reasonable care.

3. TERM AND SURVIVAL
This Agreement remains in effect for two (2) years from the effective date. Confidentiality covenants shall survive for three (3) years post-termination.

4. REMEDIES AND JURISDICTION
Parties acknowledge that unauthorized disclosure causes irreparable harm. Discloser may seek injunctive relief in addition to monetary damages in any court of competent jurisdiction.
